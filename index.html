About

One Hundred Billions - LABS

One Hundred Billions - LABS is a South African Innovation lab where we build, test and experiment with ideas that bring solutions to real problems. Presenting the LabFlyer, a DIY Wooden Drone kit where kids can build their own quadcopter & enjoy the thrill of that first take off while learning the basics of aerodynamics and electronics. LabFlyer is hands-on STEM learning, and a great way to introduce kids into AI, automation and robotics — the technology behind modern drones. We inspire kids to build today, so they can engineer tomorrow.
